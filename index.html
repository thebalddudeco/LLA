Photography License and Model Release Agreement

Current Terms

This Photography License and Model Release Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Justin Von Braun ("Photographer") and the person identified as "Model." If The Bald Dude Co. is involved in producing, publishing, or administering the shoot, references to Photographer include The Bald Dude Co. only for the rights and obligations expressly stated in this Agreement.

01

Definitions

a.
"Photographs" means still photographs created during the shoot, including edited and unedited versions.
b.
"Final Images" means edited photographs selected and delivered by Photographer for use by Model.
c.
"Raw Files" or "Unedited Images" means source files, proofs, outtakes, or images not delivered as Final Images.
d.
"Content" means photographs, video, audio, behind-the-scenes material, captions, metadata, and derivative edits created from the shoot.
e.
"Sensitive Content" means nude, semi-nude, implied nude, lingerie, fetish, sexually suggestive, vulnerable, medical, private-location, or otherwise sensitive material.
f.
"Self-Promotional Use" means Model's personal, portfolio, career, casting, press, social-media, agency, comp-card, resume, website, and editorial-profile use that promotes Model rather than a third-party product or service.
02

Ownership and Personal Rights

a.
Photographer retains copyright ownership in the Photographs and Content unless a separate written assignment is signed.
b.
Model retains all rights in Model's name, portrait, picture, likeness, voice, biography, privacy, publicity, reputation, and personal identity except for the consent and licenses expressly granted in this Agreement.
c.
No party receives rights by implication. Rights not expressly granted remain with the party that owns or controls them.
03

Rights Granted to Model

a.
Photographer grants Model a non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free license to use the Final Images for Self-Promotional Use.
b.
Model may post, display, print, submit, and reproduce the Final Images for personal websites, social-media profiles, modeling platforms, portfolios, comp cards, resumes, casting submissions, agency or manager submissions, interviews, non-paid editorial features, and similar career uses.
c.
Model may allow Model's agent, manager, publicist, casting representative, makeup artist, hair stylist, wardrobe stylist, designer, or publication to display Final Images solely to promote Model or the shoot team, provided no separate stock, merchandise, endorsement, or advertising license is granted.
d.
Model may not edit, alter, modify, crop, resize, retouch, filter, color-correct, add text or graphics, remove watermarks or metadata, use AI enhancements or generative edits, or otherwise change any Photographs or Final Images in any way, shape, or form without Photographer's prior written approval. Model may only share the Final Images exactly as delivered by Photographer.
e.
Model may request additional Final Images, high-resolution versions, alternate crops, resized files, watermarked or unwatermarked versions, or other photographer-prepared deliverables, but Photographer is not obligated to provide additional deliverables unless separately agreed in writing. Model has no right to receive, access, inspect, download, copy, or use Raw Files, unedited images, proofs, outtakes, contact sheets, source files, project files, or working files. Photographer does not provide Raw Files.
f.
Model may not sell the Photographs as stock imagery, mint or sell them as NFTs, license them for third-party advertising, place them on merchandise, use them in paid product endorsements, or grant broad sublicenses without Photographer's prior written consent.
04

Rights Granted to Photographer

a.
Model grants Photographer permission to photograph, film, record, edit, publish, display, distribute, and otherwise use Model's name, likeness, image, voice, and approved Content for Photographer's portfolio, website, social media, printed portfolio, exhibition, contest, editorial, behind-the-scenes, and promotion of Photographer's photography services.
b.
Photographer may not use Model's likeness to imply that Model endorses a third-party brand, product, service, political position, medical treatment, adult service, or controversial cause without Model's separate written consent.
c.
Photographer may not license or sell Content to a third-party advertiser, stock platform, merchandise seller, adult platform, AI dataset, or brand campaign without Model's separate written approval and any additional compensation agreed by the parties.
d.
Photographer's capture and use of Sensitive Content is governed by Section 10 and the responsible-use restrictions in this Agreement.
05

Credit and Attribution

a.
Model will use reasonable efforts to credit Photographer as "Photo by Justin Von Braun" or another credit line Photographer provides when posting Final Images online.
b.
Photographer will use reasonable efforts to identify Model by Model's preferred professional name or handle when Photographer posts Content and Model requests such credit.
c.
A missing credit caused by platform limits, third-party reposts, automatic cropping, character limits, or an accidental omission is not a breach if corrected within a reasonable time after notice.
06

Approval, Boundaries, and Takedown Process

a.
Model may communicate boundaries before or during the shoot and may decline or stop any pose, wardrobe, location, concept, audio recording, or video recording that Model is uncomfortable with. Photographer will respect those boundaries.
b.
For Sensitive Content, Photographer will provide Model a reasonable review opportunity before first public release. Model may object in writing to specific Sensitive Content based on privacy, safety, nudity, misrepresentation, or reputational concerns, and the parties will work in good faith to select approved images.
c.
Either party may request removal of Content from channels controlled by the other party if the Content is being used outside this Agreement, reveals private information, creates a safety concern, violates a platform rule, or was published by mistake. The receiving party will use commercially reasonable efforts to remove or correct the Content within ten business days, or sooner when urgent safety or privacy concerns exist.
d.
Neither party is responsible for lawful third-party reposts or platform caching outside that party's control, but both parties will cooperate in reasonable takedown requests where they have practical ability to help.
07

Responsible Use Restrictions

a.
Neither party may use the Content in a false, misleading, defamatory, harassing, hateful, discriminatory, unlawful, or exploitative manner.
b.
Neither party may create or authorize deepfakes, non-consensual nude or sexual edits, biometric identification datasets, AI training datasets, or deceptive synthetic media using the other party's identity, voice, likeness, or work without separate written consent.
c.
Neither party may remove copyright notices, watermarks, metadata, or attribution from files in a way that falsely claims authorship or interferes with the other party's rights, except that Photographer may provide unwatermarked files for permitted Model use.
d.
Neither party may publish private contact information, legal names not intended for public use, identification documents, addresses, unreleased behind-the-scenes material, or private communications without consent.
08

Model Responsibilities

a.
Model represents that Model is at least 18 years old, has provided accurate identifying information, and has authority to sign this Agreement.
b.
Model will arrive prepared for the agreed shoot, communicate scheduling or wardrobe issues promptly, follow reasonable studio, venue, and safety rules, and avoid conduct that endangers people or property.
c.
Model will use Final Images within the license granted in this Agreement, provide reasonable credit where practical, avoid misleading endorsements, and not grant rights to third parties beyond the permitted Self-Promotional Use.
d.
Model will promptly notify Photographer if Model becomes aware of unauthorized commercial exploitation, impersonation, deepfake use, or other misuse of the Content.
e.
Model is responsible for securing permission from Model's own agency, manager, employer, sponsor, wardrobe provider, tattoo artist, or other third party if their rules affect Model's participation or use of the Final Images.
09

Photographer Responsibilities

a.
Photographer will use reasonable professional care in planning, capturing, editing, storing, and delivering the Final Images.
b.
Photographer will maintain a respectful shoot environment, avoid coercion, honor agreed boundaries, and stop or adjust the shoot if Model withdraws consent for a pose, wardrobe state, location, or concept.
c.
Photographer will deliver the agreed Final Images within a reasonable time or within any separate written delivery schedule agreed by the parties, subject to ordinary editing and production constraints.
d.
Photographer will use reasonable safeguards for unreleased Content, Raw Files, identification documents, contact information, and Sensitive Content.
e.
Photographer will not knowingly publish Content in a context that is materially different from the approved shoot concept or that falsely suggests Model's endorsement of a product, service, political position, medical treatment, adult service, or controversial cause.
10

Nude, Semi-Nude, and Sensitive Content

a.
Model acknowledges and agrees that the shoot may involve nude, semi-nude, implied nude, erotic, intimate, or otherwise Sensitive Content and that this Agreement constitutes Model's informed, voluntary, written consent for Photographer to capture such Content as part of the agreed photography, video, audio, and related content-production work. Model affirms that Model is at least 18 years old.
b.
Consent to capture Sensitive Content does not authorize every possible use. Photographer may use Sensitive Content only within the rights granted in this Agreement and may not use it for third-party advertising, stock licensing, merchandise, adult-platform distribution, AI datasets, paid endorsements, or materially different contexts without Model's separate written consent.
c.
Model may withdraw consent for unpublished Sensitive Content by written notice. For Sensitive Content already published through Photographer's controlled channels, Photographer will stop future controlled uses and use commercially reasonable efforts to remove or restrict the Content after notice, subject to existing lawful third-party uses and technical limitations outside Photographer's control.
d.
Nothing in this Agreement waives claims for breach of this Agreement, intentional misconduct, coercion, fraud, gross negligence, unauthorized sensitive publication, unlawful use, or uses outside the written consent given.
e.
If the shoot involves actual or simulated sexually explicit conduct or other material subject to age-verification, recordkeeping, labeling, or custodian-of-records requirements, the parties will comply with applicable federal and state law, including 18 U.S.C. Sections 2257 and 2257A where applicable.
11

Video, Audio, and Behind-the-Scenes Material

a.
Video, audio, livestream, and behind-the-scenes material may be captured as part of the agreed photography and content-production work. Model may request that private conversations, dressing areas, rest periods, or sensitive setup moments not be recorded.
b.
Permitted video, audio, and behind-the-scenes Content may be used under the same balanced rights, restrictions, consent limits, and takedown process that apply to Photographs.
c.
Photographer retains creative control over editing and final presentation, but will not edit Content in a way that is intentionally misleading, humiliating, defamatory, or outside the approved concept.
d.
Commercial distribution of video or audio beyond portfolio, editorial, exhibition, contest, social-media, or promotion of Photographer's photography services requires a separate written agreement.
12

Compensation, Expenses, and Deliverables

a.
Compensation, trade-for-portfolio terms, expense reimbursement, deliverables, delivery timing, retouching scope, usage upgrades, and any paid commercial use should be stated in a separate signed writing, booking confirmation, invoice, message, or other written agreement between the parties.
b.
Unless a separate payment obligation is stated, the rights granted in this Agreement are exchanged for the shoot participation, creative services, images, and other consideration described by the parties.
c.
No additional compensation is owed for permitted portfolio, self-promotional, editorial, social-media, exhibition, contest, or photography-service promotional uses. Paid third-party advertising, endorsement, adult-platform distribution, stock licensing, merchandise, or other expanded commercial exploitation requires a separate written agreement.
13

Term, Termination, and Breach

a.
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and remains in effect for approved uses unless terminated by mutual written agreement, court order, or uncured material breach.
b.
If either party materially breaches this Agreement, the non-breaching party will provide written notice describing the breach. The breaching party will have ten business days to cure if the breach can reasonably be cured.
c.
Termination for breach stops new uses by the breaching party that depend on this Agreement. Termination does not automatically require removal of previously lawful, non-sensitive portfolio or self-promotional uses unless removal is required by Section 6, Section 10, court order, or a written settlement.
d.
Sections concerning ownership, permitted prior uses, responsible-use restrictions, confidentiality of private information, takedown cooperation, indemnification, dispute resolution, and governing law survive termination.
14

Mutual Indemnification and Liability

a.
Each party is responsible for that party's own breach of this Agreement, unauthorized grants of rights, unlawful conduct, negligence, willful misconduct, and violations of third-party rights.
b.
Model will indemnify Photographer for claims caused by Model's unauthorized commercial licensing, misleading endorsements, breach of Model responsibilities, or inaccurate representations.
c.
Photographer will indemnify Model for claims caused by Photographer's unauthorized publication, unauthorized commercial licensing, breach of Photographer responsibilities, or use of Content outside the consent granted by Model.
d.
Neither party is required to indemnify the other for the other party's own breach, negligence, willful misconduct, unauthorized use, or unlawful acts.
15

Dispute Resolution and Governing Law

a.
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-law rules.
b.
Before filing a lawsuit, the parties will attempt in good faith to resolve disputes through direct written communication and, if practical, mediation in New York. Either party may seek urgent injunctive relief for privacy, safety, sensitive-content, intellectual-property, or unauthorized-commercial-use concerns.
c.
The parties acknowledge that New York law recognizes written consent requirements for certain uses of a living person's name, portrait, picture, likeness, or voice for advertising or trade purposes. This Agreement is intended to document the written consent granted and the limits of that consent.
16

Miscellaneous

a.
This Agreement and any written amendment contain the entire agreement between the parties about the shoot and Content.
b.
Amendments, usage upgrades, waivers, approvals, and sensitive-content permissions must be in writing and signed, emailed, texted, or otherwise clearly confirmed by the party granting the permission.
c.
If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain effective and the unenforceable provision will be limited to the minimum extent necessary.
d.
Electronic signatures and counterparts are acceptable.
